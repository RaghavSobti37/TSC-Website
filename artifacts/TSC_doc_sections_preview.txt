Hero Eyebrow
ABOUT US
Hero Title
The Shakti Collective
Hero Tagline
Unfolding Artist Force 
Hero Headline
Building India’s Culture-Forward Artist Ecosystem
Hero Body
The Shakti Collective is a culture-forward artist ecosystem company built to discover, develop and scale artists, stories and cultural movements.
We work across music, films, learning, live experiences, artist partnerships, original IPs and culture-led collaborations — creating the systems artists need to grow, the stories audiences want to believe in, and the cultural properties that can travel from India to the world.
Core Belief
Artists do not need only exposure.
They need ecosystems.
Who We Are
A Music & Story Artists Company
The Shakti Collective brings together the worlds of music, stories, artists, films, learning, live experiences and culture under one integrated ecosystem.
We partner with independent artists, musicians, storytellers, filmmakers, brands, institutions and creative communities to build work that is emotionally resonant, culturally relevant and commercially sustainable.
TSC is not built as a conventional talent management company, production house, academy or agency.
It is built as a new-age culture company — one that helps artists and stories move from potential to possibility, and from possibility to impact.
Why We Exist
Because Talent Needs Direction
Today, artists have more access than ever before. They can record from home, release independently, build communities online and reach audiences without waiting for traditional gatekeepers.
But access alone does not build a career. Many artists still struggle with identity, positioning, craft development, audience building, live opportunities, industry access, monetisation and long-term direction.
Similarly, powerful stories often struggle to find the right audience, cultural positioning and partner ecosystem that can help them travel.
The Shakti Collective exists to close this gap. We build the bridge between talent and opportunity, stories and audiences, artists and culture, creative expression and sustainable growth.
Vision
To Build the World’s Most Trusted Culture Company
Our vision is to build the world’s most trusted culture company by empowering artists to create cultural movements that enrich millions of lives.
Our long-term mission is to serve 10 million artists and take 100 stories to the globe.
We believe India is entering a defining cultural moment. The next wave of global entertainment from India will not come only from content. It will come from artists, stories, communities, values, music, mythology, lived experience and cultural imagination.
The Shakti Collective is being built for that future.
Philosophy
Conscious Entertainment for a New Generation
We believe entertainment can be powerful without being empty. It can be contemporary and still be rooted. It can be popular and still have depth. It can be commercially successful and still carry meaning. It can travel globally without losing its soul.
Our work is guided by the journey of:
Human → Artist → Art → Audience → Community → Culture → Opportunity → Sustainable Career
Before an artist becomes a performer, they are a human being. Before art becomes content, it is an expression. Before a story becomes a product, it is a living emotion. Before culture becomes business, it is a shared experience.
This is the foundation of TSC.
What We Build - Overview
Interconnected Verticals. One Ecosystem.
TSC Artists
Culture-forward artist partnerships
TSC Artists is our artist partnership platform focused on helping independent artists build long-term creative, cultural and commercial journeys. Our work includes identity, positioning, repertoire, live opportunities, collaborations, brand partnerships, audience development, visibility and career growth.
TSC Academy
Learning, mentorship and incubation for serious artists
TSC Academy is our learning and development platform for artists, musicians, composers, singers, producers and creative professionals. Through structured courses, masterclasses, mentorship, community, resources and industry exposure, we help artists build clarity, confidence and craft.
The Artist Path
A curated artist accelerator
The Artist Path is a selective artist accelerator curated by Rohith Sobti for artists ready to share their craft with audiences and build a serious professional journey. It works across discovery, identity, repertoire, release strategy, live readiness, audience building and long-term roadmap creation.
TSC Films
Mounting films. Building audiences. Creating lasting IPs.
TSC Films works with filmmakers, producers and studios to build audience demand, cultural relevance and commercial opportunities around films. We support stories through film mounting, audience research, positioning, community building, partnerships, release planning, monetisation and long-term IP growth.
TSC Originals
Creating cultural IPs and living story worlds
TSC Originals develops cultural properties across music, live experiences, youth culture, spiritual storytelling, digital formats and community-led entertainment. Our IPs are designed as living ecosystems that can grow across platforms, communities, partnerships and global stages.
TSC Collaborations
Brands, institutions, governments and culture partners
TSC collaborates with brands, institutions, governments and cultural partners to build meaningful creative platforms, artist-led campaigns, community initiatives and culture-first experiences that feel authentic, emotionally resonant and valuable to real communities.
Ecosystem Model
Discover — Identify artists, stories, sounds, ideas and cultural opportunities with emotional and audience potential.
Develop — Shape craft, identity, positioning, confidence, repertoire, storytelling and professional readiness.
Curate — Bring creative direction, strategic thinking, business understanding and cultural sensitivity into the process.
Launch — Activate artists, films and IPs through music, live experiences, digital platforms, partnerships, campaigns and communities.
Scale — Build long-term audience growth, fandom, revenue, cultural relevance and global opportunities.
Who We Work With
Artists — We help artists discover identity, sharpen craft, build audiences and create sustainable creative careers.
Filmmakers & Storytellers — We help stories find their audience, positioning, partnerships, cultural relevance and monetisation pathways.
Brands — We create meaningful culture-led partnerships that go beyond advertising and connect with real communities.
Institutions & Governments — We collaborate on learning, incubation, talent development, cultural programming and creative ecosystem building.
Audiences — We create experiences that entertain, inspire, connect and stay with people.
Belief System
Artist-first. Ecosystem-led. Artists need long-term partners, not short-term campaigns.
Culture-forward. We build work that carries emotional, social and cultural relevance.
Rooted and global. Indian artists, stories, sounds and philosophies can travel the world with confidence.
Conscious and contemporary. Depth and scale can co-exist.
Business with meaning. Creative businesses should create value for artists, audiences, partners and society.
Founder Bio
Rohith Sobti
Founder & Curator, The Shakti Collective
Rohith Sobti is an entertainment strategist with nearly three decades of experience across music, films, artist development, brand partnerships and cultural IP creation.
Over the years, he has held leadership roles at Yash Raj Films, Sony Music India and Universal Music India, working with some of India’s most recognised artists, music properties, films and entertainment brands.
His work has consistently focused on building artist-first ecosystems, creating meaningful partnerships and helping creative talent grow with clarity, purpose and scale.
With The Shakti Collective, Rohit brings together his experience across the entertainment industry to build a culture-forward company that empowers artists, nurtures original stories and creates opportunities that generate long-term cultural and commercial impact.

The future of entertainment will not be shaped by content alone. It will be shaped by artists with a clear identity, stories that carry meaning, communities that believe in them, and ecosystems that help creativity thrive.
The Shakti Collective was born from a simple belief that extraordinary talent deserves more than visibility. It deserves guidance, opportunity, collaboration and a long-term ecosystem that enables sustainable growth.
Our vision is to discover artists, nurture stories, build cultural IPs and create experiences that connect deeply with audiences. When artists are empowered with the right support, they do not just build successful careers. They shape conversations, inspire communities and move culture forward.
Every initiative at TSC — whether in music, films, learning, live experiences, original IPs or collaborations — is driven by this purpose.
This is our commitment. This is The Shakti Collective.

Hero Headline
Mounting Films. Building Audiences. Creating Lasting IPs.
Hero Body
A film deserves more than a successful release.
It deserves an audience that believes in it, partners that strengthen it and a strategy that allows it to grow long after the opening weekend.
That is where TSC Films comes in.
We work with filmmakers, producers and studios to strategically mount films from the early stages of their journey. By combining audience strategy, cultural positioning, partnerships and monetisation, we help transform films into entertainment properties with long-term value.
What We Build
Film Mounting — Building the strategic foundation that prepares a film for the market before it reaches the market.
Audience Development — Creating awareness, anticipation and meaningful engagement long before release.
Strategic Partnerships — Bringing together brands, institutions, communities and collaborators that add long-term value.
Monetisation & IP Growth — Unlocking opportunities beyond the theatrical journey.
Franchise Development — Helping stories evolve into long-term entertainment ecosystems.
Our Process
Discover — Understand the story, audience and potential.
Position — Define the film’s identity, audience and cultural relevance.
Build — Develop partnerships, communities and audience demand.
Launch — Support release through integrated audience and partnership strategies.
Grow — Extend the life of the film through monetisation, community and long-term IP development.
Why TSC Films
Most films begin thinking about the audience when marketing begins.
We believe the audience should be part of the conversation from day one.
We do not see films as campaigns. We see them as intellectual properties capable of creating communities, partnerships, experiences and long-term cultural impact.
Our role is simple: to help great stories find the audiences they deserve and create value far beyond the box office.
