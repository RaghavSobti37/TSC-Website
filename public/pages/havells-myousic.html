01 / Founding Vision

A real platform for artists with talent, but limited access.

Across India, thousands of independent artists are writing songs, composing melodies, singing, producing music and dreaming of building a life through their craft. Many are serious and deserving, but lack access to mentors, collaborators, resources and professional guidance.

The founding vision of Tarsem Mittal and the Havells leadership team was to create a meaningful platform for those artists. The Shakti Collective transformed that vision into a 360-degree artist discovery, mentorship and development initiative.

02 / TSC Contribution

Designed to serve the artist first.

  • Built and executed the platform across Greater Noida, Indore and Dumka.
  • Designed the artist journey from registration to audition to mentorship.
  • Created a respectful audition environment focused on dignity, feedback and learning.
  • Curated mentors, performers, artists and industry professionals.

03 / Ecosystem

Conversations over competition. Learning over elimination.

Artists participated in auditions, open mic sessions, mentor interactions, knowledge-sharing sessions, live performances and peer conversations. The goal was to ensure that every artist walked away with feedback, confidence, clarity, relationships or motivation to continue creating.

04 / Next Phase

The goal is not just to release music. The goal is to build artists.

Selected artists move into an intensive development journey where musicians, songwriters, composers and producers collaborate to create and release 25 original songs.

Each release is planned with support across songwriting, composition, music production, branding, content creation, distribution, marketing, PR and launch strategy.

Success will be measured by artists who look back and say: this is where my journey truly began.
