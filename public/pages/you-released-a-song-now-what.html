You Released a Song. Now What?

A practical guide for independent artists to turn a release into listeners, fans, learning, and momentum.

Rohit Sobti Jul 27, 2026 12 min read on Medium

This local version presents the guide as a TSC editorial preview. The full article remains on Medium.

Read on Medium

The Core Idea

Release day is not the finish line. It is the beginning of a feedback loop: who heard the song, what moved them, where they came from, and what the artist can do next to deepen the relationship.

"A Practical Guide for Independent Artists to Build Listeners, Fans and Momentum"

The guide reframes promotion as a sequence of repeatable actions instead of a one-time announcement. The work after release is where the song starts meeting real people.

What Artists Should Build

Independent artists need a system around the song: clear storytelling, short-form assets, audience touchpoints, performance data, collaborations, and a way to keep listeners close after the first stream.

  • Turn the release story into multiple pieces of content instead of a single post.
  • Watch listener behavior and use it to decide where to spend effort next.
  • Build momentum through consistency, not panic-posting in the first week.

TSC Lens

The Shakti Collective treats release strategy as artist development. A song needs visibility, but it also needs context. The more clearly an artist can explain the world around the song, the easier it becomes for listeners to enter it and stay.
