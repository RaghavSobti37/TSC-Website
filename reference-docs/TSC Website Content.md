    **The Problem: The Meteor Effect**
    *   **Concept:** Most artists today are Meteors — brilliant flashes of talent that fade before they can become North Stars. The industry is designed for the 1%, leaving the 99% without a compass.
    *   **Stats:**
        *   30% Independent artists in streaming top charts.
        *   70% Established artists dominate revenue.
        *   1 in 1000 Viral artists sustain a career.

    **The Solution: The Roundway (5 Stage Ecosystem)**
    1.  **PREPARE:** Build your foundation. Industry-led mentorship and deep-dive technical training.
    2.  **CREATE:** Find your voice. Collaborative spaces and cross-discipline creation.
    3.  **PRODUCE:** Make it real. Access to infrastructure, funding, and global production standards.
    4.  **MONETIZE:** Turn craft into commerce. Strategic brand partnerships and IP licensing.
    5.  **REPLICATE:** Go global. Scaling your impact and replicating success across territories.
