Building a Cultural Movement Around a Timeless Story

A cultural resonance initiative celebrating Lord Jagannath’s living tradition through cinema, community, devotion and new-generation storytelling.

Mahaprabhu Jagannath already has a living audience — devotees, families, communities and cultural institutions. The opportunity was not to introduce the story, but to create new pathways for a new generation to experience it.

TSC supported audience discovery, cultural relevance mapping, community-led engagement, strategic partnerships, audience growth frameworks and long-term ecosystem thinking.
