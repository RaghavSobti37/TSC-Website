# Sadhu Tiwari

URL: https://theshakticollective.in/sadhu-tiwari

Singer | Composer | Lyricist | Music Director

Bringing India's devotional heritage and regional folk soul into contemporary cinema and live performance, Sadhu Tiwari creates music rooted in tradition yet contemporary in expression. With over 15 years in the industry, he has worked across Hindi cinema, independent music and devotional compositions.

## Highlights

- Best Music Director, Jagran Film Festival Awards 2026 (Hanuman Ansh)
- "Jab Zid Par Aa Gayi Parvati" (Hanuman Ansh): 364M+ plays on YouTube Music; No. 1 on India's YouTube Daily Music Charts, No. 2 globally
- 418M+ plays across his five Hanuman Ansh songs: Parvati, Aavagaman, Hey Kharari, Bajrang Baanka, Saasa Haale
- Performed "Parvati" at Bhajan Clubbing, New Delhi, with Prime Minister Narendra Modi on the khanjari
- Performed "Awadhiya Ke Raja" (Nayyi Navelli) at Ram Ki Paidi, Ayodhya

## Selected work

Hanuman Ansh (2026), Luv Ki Arrange Marriage (2024), Kisko Tha Pata (2024), Kuch Khattaa Ho Jaay (2024), Janhit Mein Jaari (2022), Dhaage (2022), Jeena Bhool Jaunga (2021), Tujhse Juda Dil (2021), The Accidental Prime Minister (2019), Gulaab Gang (2014).
