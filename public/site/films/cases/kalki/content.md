# Kalki

URL: https://theshakticollective.in/kalki

## Copy

Building Anticipation Around the Story of Tomorrow

A culture-forward storytelling initiative exploring the story of tomorrow through ancient Indian imagination and modern audience engagement.

Kalki represents transformation, justice and the renewal of dharma. The opportunity was to position it not just as another mythology film, but as a cinematic experience that speaks equally to tradition and the future.

TSC supported audience positioning, cultural branding, audience development, community engagement, strategic partnerships and growth planning.
