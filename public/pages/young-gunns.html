01 / Opportunity

India has never lacked musical talent. It has lacked structured artist development.

Thousands of musicians release independent songs every year, yet very few receive professional guidance on songwriting, production, branding, visual identity, audience building or long-term career strategy.

The Young Gunns was created to invest in artists at the beginning of their journey, when guidance can change the trajectory of a career.

02 / Approach

The focus was not competition. It was growth.

The Young Gunns was designed as a collaborative incubation programme where selected musicians worked closely with industry professionals across every stage of the creative process.

Each participant was encouraged to develop their artistic identity while learning the realities of recording, collaboration, production and release.

  • Programme conceptualisation, artist selection and curation.
  • Creative direction, music curation and artist development.
  • Production planning, visual identity and campaign strategy.
  • Content creation, release planning and community building.

03 / Ecosystem

Great artists are built through great teams.

The programme featured music curation by Vasav Vashisht, while visual identity was developed through creative collaborations led by Deepak V. Rawat and Winston.

Behind the scenes, producers, engineers, assistants and creative collaborators worked together to ensure every artist experienced the process of developing professional-quality music and visual storytelling.

The Young Gunns was not created to release a collection of songs. It was created to introduce a new generation of artists to the industry.

04 / Looking Ahead

A sustainable launchpad for independent talent.

The Young Gunns is envisioned as an ongoing artist incubation platform that will continue identifying and nurturing India's next generation of original musicians.

Future seasons can expand through larger cohorts, new creative collaborations, live showcases, mentorship opportunities and original music releases.

Believe in artists before the world does.
