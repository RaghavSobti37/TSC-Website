01 / Overview

Behind every notebook is a creator waiting to be heard.

Main Bhi Artist was born from a simple observation: while millions of people write every day, very few find the confidence or platform to share their work.

Co-created by Laksh Maheshwari and The Shakti Collective, it brings together aspiring writers, poets, lyricists and storytellers from India and around the world.

02 / Challenge

A trusted ecosystem, not another creator page.

The challenge was to create a space where people felt safe sharing deeply personal work. The initiative had to balance creative freedom with quality, encourage participation without intimidation and foster meaningful interactions instead of superficial engagement.

Every artist deserves a stage before they deserve a spotlight.

03 / What We Built
