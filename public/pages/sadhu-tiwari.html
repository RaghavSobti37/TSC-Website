Who Is Sadhu Tiwari?

Rooted · Devotional · Cinematic

Sadhu Tiwari, also credited as Sadhu S. Tiwari and Sadhu Sushil Tiwari, is an Indian playback singer, composer, lyricist and music director. His musical language brings together classical influences, regional folk traditions and contemporary Indian sounds.

With over 15 years in the music industry, he has built a distinctive identity across Hindi cinema, independent music and devotional compositions, moving naturally between cinematic storytelling and live musical expression.

Roots

Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh

Born in Karahiya village near Gwalior, he began singing on local Ramlila stages. He earned a Master’s in Applied Biochemistry, trained under his guru Sanjay Devle, and taught Biochemistry and Chemistry for nearly four years before choosing music full time.

Mumbai & Cinema

Since 2010

He moved to Mumbai in 2010 and assisted composer Sandesh Shandilya. His breakthrough came with “Mauj Ki Malhare” in Gulaab Gang (2014), beginning a long creative association with filmmaker Dr. Vishal Chaturvedi. In 2026 he wrote, composed and sang five songs for Hanuman Ansh.

For Sadhu, music does not need excess to make an impact. Authenticity, simplicity and emotional honesty sit at the heart of every composition.
