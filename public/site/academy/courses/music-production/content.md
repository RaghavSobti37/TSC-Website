# A-Z of Music Production

URL: https://wix-site-clone-psi.vercel.app/music-production

## Copy

Course: A-Z of Music Production

Mentor: Luca Petracca

A practical international masterclass in end-to-end track creation, built for artists who want to produce professional music using only a laptop.

This practical learning program by Mr. Luca Petracca teaches artists how to take an idea to a finished track: crafting melodies, understanding chord progressions and harmonic functions, shaping sounds with virtual instruments, arranging common song forms across genres, recording clean takes with laptop-friendly gear, and applying production, FX, mixing and mastering techniques.

Modules cover essential topics with hands-on projects so learners can produce professional-sounding songs without expensive studio gear.

Quote: "I believe I have found my answers, but I am not here to share them with you. I am here to help you find the right questions. With this, let's dive into the A-Z of Music Production."

Hi, I am Luca Petracca, a music producer and composer from Italy. I learned classical guitar from the Conservatory S. Cecilia of Rome before moving to the Netherlands to study classical composition at the Conservatorium van Amsterdam. Traveling across the globe, I have been teaching music for more than 17 years and have produced multiple tracks.

Highlights: Music Producer & Composer | Classical Guitar & Composition | 17+ Years Teaching | Laptop-Based Production
