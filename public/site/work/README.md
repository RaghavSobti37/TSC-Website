## Source copy (verbatim)

- Building platforms, partnerships and cultural movements that create lasting impact.
- At The Shakti Collective, every initiative is designed to create meaningful opportunities for artists, stories and communities. Through collaborations with brands, creators, filmmakers and industry leaders, we build platforms that discover talent, nurture creativity and shape the future of India’s cultural ecosystem.
- Main Bhi Artist — Every Artist Has a Story. A global creator community built for writers, musicians, poets, storytellers and artists to share their work, find their audience and grow together.
- Havells mYOUsic — Discovering India’s Next Musical Voices. A grassroots music movement created to discover, mentor and launch emerging singers, composers, lyricists and instrumentalists.
- Insta Music League — Where Original Music Takes Centre Stage. A digital-first music discovery format built to celebrate originality over popularity metrics.
- The Young Gunns — Building the Next Generation of Artists. An artist incubation programme where emerging musicians collaborate with experienced producers and creative mentors.
- TSC Films — Mounting Films. Building Audiences. Creating Lasting IPs. Strategic support for independent films and culture-forward stories.
