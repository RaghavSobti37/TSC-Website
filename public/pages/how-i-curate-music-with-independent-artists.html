How I Curate Music With Independent Artists

Lessons from Lost;Found with Faheem Abdullah — why breakthroughs come from journey, belief, and mounting, not only more releases.

Rohit Sobti Jul 29, 2026 14 min read on Medium

This local version presents the essay as a TSC editorial preview. The full article remains on Medium.

Read on Medium

The Core Idea

Independent artists often keep releasing songs, videos, and collabs. A breakthrough rarely comes from volume alone. It comes when songs form a journey — when sound, visuals, collaborators, audience signals, timing, and belief move in one direction.

"Good music releases songs. Great curation reveals the artist."

Music curation is not only track selection. It is understanding the artist deeply enough to know which songs become their bridge to the world — as the Lost;Found journey with Faheem Abdullah showed.

What Artists Can Take Away

The essay walks through Faheem’s path from early signals like Jhelum, through album shaping, Sajde, the Srinagar launch, and Ishq — then extracts a practical playbook for independents.

  • Find your why before you release more songs.
  • Study audience signals and build a body of work, not only singles.
  • Collaborate deeply, mount the music properly, stay rooted, and trust the right team.

TSC Lens

The Shakti Collective treats curation as revelation, not control. The artist already carries the world. The work is to listen, shape the concept, protect what is sacred, and mount it so that truth can travel — with belief as the real success factor.
