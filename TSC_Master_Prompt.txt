# THE SHAKTI COLLECTIVE - MASTER FRONTEND PROMPT

## 1. BRAND IDENTITY & VISION
**The Shakti Collective (TSC)** is possibly the world’s first-ever original content-creation ecosystem built around empowering, mentoring, and managing talent (artistes). 
- **Verb:** UNFOLD (remove constraints and let talent emerge).
- **Mission:** A talent-first global culture-creation engine. 
- **Core Proposition:** Spot - Nurture - Nourish - Monetize.
- **Target Audience:** "Conscious Globalists" (Value original content, human perspectives, and spiritual/emotional residue).
- **Core Values:** Fearlessness, Integrity, Love for Craft, Insatiable Curiosity.
