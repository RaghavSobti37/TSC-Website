Mentor: Pt. Prasad Khaparde

Course Overview

Immerse yourself in the timeless art of Hindustani classical singing with this comprehensive learning program designed for aspiring vocalists of all levels.

Learn directly from Pandit Prasad Khaparde, a legendary Hindustani classical vocalist with over 30 years of experience, trained in the prestigious Rampur Sahaswan gharana under Padma Bhushan Ustad Rashid Khan.

 

Through 5+ exclusive live group sessions where you'll receive personalized feedback & guidance, 500+ minutes of recorded content, quality assessments & certification, this learning program is designed to develop your classical singing abilities.

Learn the sacred nuances, raag structures & vocal techniques that form the foundation of classical Indian music. This learning program takes you on a deep journey through one of the world's oldest musical traditions.

Legendary Classical Vocalist

PRASAD KHAPARDE

COURSE MENTOR

A renowned Hindustani classical vocalist of international repute with over 30 years of illustrious career, Pandit Prasad Khaparde is from the Rampur Sahaswan gharana. His musical journey began under the guidance of Padma Bhushan Ustad Rashid Khan Sahab, one of the greatest vocalists of our time. His music captivates both connoisseurs and music lovers globally.

​

In this comprehensive course, Prasad shares the sacred traditions and technical mastery he has cultivated over decades. Through live group sessions, you'll experience his teaching methodology that emphasizes both rigorous technique and spiritual connection to music. Join one of India's greatest classical vocalists on your musical journey.
