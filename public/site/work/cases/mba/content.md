Giving Every Artist a Voice

A global creator community co-created with Laksh Maheshwari to discover, celebrate and nurture emerging writers, poets, lyricists and storytellers.

Millions of creators write every day, but very few find the confidence or platform to share their work. Main Bhi Artist was built as a safe creative movement where every voice could be seen, heard and encouraged.

TSC led concept development, positioning, community strategy, creator engagement, digital platform thinking, submission flow, content planning and long-term ecosystem roadmap.

A growing creator community with thousands of members, original submissions and a digital platform designed for stories, poems, lyrics and creative expression.

Looking Ahead

Main Bhi Artist is being built as a global creator ecosystem connecting emerging voices with collaborators, mentors, opportunities and original creative projects.
